### What each route carries

- `health`: Liveness probe (response)
- `sessionGet`: The signed-in user or null, the server time and whether the data is synthetic (response)
- `sessionLogin`: Sign in with e-mail and password; sets the session cookie (body, response)
- `sessionLogout`: Sign out (body, no body (204))
- `resourcesList`: List resources; a Member may not ask for archived or all (query, response)
- `resourcesCreate`: Create a resource (body, response)
- `resourcesGet`: One resource; archived resources stay readable (params, response)
- `resourcesUpdate`: Edit a resource; an archived resource cannot be edited and status cannot be changed (params, body, response)
- `resourcesArchive`: Archive a resource that has no future pending or confirmed booking (params, body, response)
- `resourceSchedule`: Blocks of one resource for 1 to 7 Oslo days, shaped per viewer (params, query, response)
- `bookingsList`: List bookings; scope=all is for Administrators (query, response)
- `bookingsCreate`: Create a booking (confirmed at once, or pending when the resource needs approval) (body, response)
- `bookingsGet`: One booking with its events; the owner or an Administrator only (params, response)
- `bookingCancel`: Cancel a pending or confirmed booking before its start (params, body, response)
- `bookingApprove`: Approve a pending request that has not started (params, body, response)
- `bookingReject`: Reject a pending request with a reason; also works on an Expired request (params, body, response)
- `dashboardGet`: Upcoming own bookings; Administrators also get the 7-day panel (response)
- `activityList`: Activity events newest first, scoped by role (query, response)

## Raised while building Stories 1.1-1.7

5. Field-level Norwegian messages in `packages/contracts/src/errors.ts` (`fieldMessageNb`). Fixed by EXPERIENCE.md: "Maks 200 tegn." and "Maks 4 timer.". Proposed by the builder: "Må fylles ut.", "Ugyldig verdi.", "Velg et gyldig klokkeslett.", "Velg hele eller halve timer.", "Velg et tidspunkt mellom 08:00 og 22:00.", "Sluttid må være etter starttid.", "Minst 30 minutter.", "Tidspunktet har passert.". "Du kan booke inntil 30 døgn frem i tid." is approved copy.
6. `BOOKING_STARTED` details carry `action: "approve" | "cancel"` (besides `currentStatus`) so the client and the server can choose between the two approved wordings; AD-4 lists only `currentStatus`.
7. `FIELD_ERROR_CODES` (REQUIRED, TOO_LONG_TEXT, INVALID, the booking time codes, IN_PAST, BEYOND_HORIZON) are the machine codes of `fieldErrors[].code`; AD-4 leaves the field-error code vocabulary open.
8. A booked slot reads "taken" even after it has started, "past" otherwise (EXPERIENCE.md Flow 1 shows 09:00 as opptatt while 08:00 is passert).
9. Layer reading for lint (Story 1.1, `docs/stack-note.md`): `ui` may import `i18n/*` and `api/links`; `api` may import `i18n/format`; the diagram is ambiguous on these arrows.
