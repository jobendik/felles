FELLES — UX Design Validation Report

_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/ux-designs/ux-felles-pilot-2026-10-19/ · DESIGN.md + EXPERIENCE.md

Rubric walker. A usable contract, not yet strong at review time. Every {path} reference resolved, all four UJs and the E2E-implied flows had a protagonist, steps, climax and failure path, the glossary matched the sources, and no UX decision altered an approved requirement (Part A and CR-1…CR-4 honoured). What held it back were load-bearing decisions left to the implementer: the Booking detail sheet had no anatomy or states, the booking date control was never named, and the slot picker rules could not express a 30-minute booking.

Accessibility lens. Close to AA-safe but not there at review time. Every colour pair the spine specified passed (text 4.58–17.27:1, control boundaries 3.27–3.73:1, toast icons 10.96 and 8.52:1); status was never colour-only; overlays trapped and returned focus. The failures were in pairs the spine never checked (the link inside the dark toast at 2.70:1) and in behaviour left open on the core booking path (focus dropped by disabled in-flight buttons, no accessible date selection, an under-specified week-grid keyboard model).

After the fix pass (2026-10-19). Every high and medium finding below is marked with how it was resolved in the spines. One item stays open by design: the mobile reading of VIS-01 (week strip + day timeline) needs Jo's explicit decision.
